## Description

Message Saver for Messenger keeps a private copy of your own Messenger chats on your computer, so you can still read a message after the sender unsends or deletes it.

Not affiliated with, endorsed by, or made by Meta Platforms, Inc. "Messenger" and "Facebook" are trademarks of Meta.

WHAT IT DOES
• Saves the messages you receive and send on facebook.com and messenger.com while a chat is open in your browser: text, photos, stickers, and notes about voice messages and links.
• Unsent or deleted messages: the saved original appears right under Messenger's "… unsent a message" line, in a red box only you can see.
• Edited messages: earlier versions are kept and shown under the message.
• Saved Messages page: browse chats, filter unsent, edited and photos, search everything, and export as a backup, a readable web page or a spreadsheet.
• Unseen (optional, can be turned off): your read receipts ("Seen") and the "typing…" indicator are held back in normal chats. In end-to-end encrypted chats this works through "Strong mode" on facebook.com/messages and messenger.com, which tells Messenger the window is not focused. Side effect: Messenger may play its sound or show a notification while you read.
• Downloads: a "⬇" button on photos, videos and reels (and a right-click entry) saves them to your Downloads folder, in HD when Facebook offers it. Download only what you have the right to keep.
• English or Bangla (বাংলা) interface.
• Optional PIN lock, so other people who use the computer cannot open your saved messages.

YOUR DATA STAYS ON YOUR COMPUTER
• Everything is stored only in this browser on this computer. Nothing is sent to us or anyone else. There is no server, no account, no analytics and no ads.
• The saved copy is not encrypted. Anyone who can use your computer account can read it, so keep your computer locked or use the PIN lock.
• You can delete everything at any time in Settings. Removing the extension deletes it too.

LIMITS
• A message can be recovered only if it reached your browser before it was unsent: a Facebook or Messenger tab must have been open.
• In encrypted chats, the message must have been shown on screen at least once.
• Facebook changes its website often; some features may need an update after such changes.

Please respect other people's privacy: keep what you save for yourself.
