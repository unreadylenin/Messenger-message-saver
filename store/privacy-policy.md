# Privacy policy: Message Saver for Messenger (Messenger Message Saver)

Last updated: 23 November 2026

This browser extension keeps a private copy of your own Messenger chats on your computer. This policy explains what it handles and where that data goes. In short: **it stays on your computer**.

## What the extension handles
While you use facebook.com or messenger.com in the browser, the extension reads Messenger data and the chat shown on screen. It then saves:
- message text, time and sender name of the chats you have open, including messages later unsent or edited, and earlier versions of edited messages;
- photos and stickers in those chats (unless you turn this off), and short notes about voice messages, videos and links;
- copies of Messenger's own desktop notifications (sender and text, unless you turn this off);
- your settings, simple counters (for example how many "Seen" signals were held back), and, if you set one, a hash of your PIN (never the PIN itself).

## Where it is stored
Only on your computer, inside the browser's storage for this extension (IndexedDB and the extension's settings storage). It is **not encrypted**, so anyone who can use your computer account or browser profile can read it. The optional PIN lock keeps other people who use the computer out of the extension's pages; it does not encrypt the data.

## What is sent, and to whom
**Nothing is sent to the developer or to any third party.** The extension has no server, no account, no analytics, no advertising and no remote code. It contacts only Facebook's own servers, and only to:
- download photos from Facebook's image servers (`fbcdn.net`), so they stay visible after an unsend;
- download the photo or video you ask for with the download button, and if needed read that video's own Facebook page to find its file.

The extension does not sell, share or transfer your data, does not use it for advertising or to judge creditworthiness, and does not use it for any purpose other than the features described here.

## Keeping and deleting
- Data is kept until you delete it. In Settings you can delete one chat, delete everything, or delete messages older than a number of days automatically.
- Removing the extension from the browser deletes all of its stored data.
- Files you export or download (backups, web pages, spreadsheets, photos, videos) are ordinary files on your computer, and you delete them yourself.

## Other people's messages
Saved chats contain other people's messages, including ones they unsent. Please keep them private and use them lawfully.

## Children
The extension is not directed at children.

## Changes
If the extension ever starts handling data differently, this policy and the extension's own welcome page will say so before it happens.
